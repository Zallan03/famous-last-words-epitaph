# PROMPT — Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph

Write the funniest possible one-line famous last words as a single
tombstone epitaph, at most 150 characters, saved to /output/entry.txt.

The line must be, in the judge's order of importance:

1. A one-line tombstone epitaph of famous last words — a single line of
   dying speech carved in stone.
2. Genuinely funny on one read — a real joke, not a mild pun or a sad
   sentiment.
3. Built on a surprise, a twist, or a double meaning that lands cleanly.
4. Short and stone-like — no stage directions, no explanation.
5. Quotable enough to repeat to a friend.

Avoid the patterns the field already uses: "I died doing what I loved:
ignoring X", "I died as I lived: ...", the Wi-Fi password dying with the
speaker, "hold my beer / watch this", the manager, one-star reviews of
death, warning labels, terms and conditions, the check-engine light.
Find an angle nobody has used: a double meaning that only lands as
dying speech.

The entry I wrote from this prompt:

> I left my body to science. Science left it on read.
